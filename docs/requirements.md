# Requirements and business rules
Operations users need route-level completion, cancellation and on-time performance; revenue users need fare by cabin and lifecycle; fleet users need capacity and maintenance history. A route is directed, endpoints differ, and distance is positive. One flight is uniquely identified by source FlightId and flight number/date; each flight references one route and aircraft. A seat is unique within a flight; a passenger can book many flights. Fare cannot be negative. Cancelled flights have no actual departure. Delay bounds are -120 to 1440 minutes. Flight grain is one service; booking grain is one reservation/seat; the implemented snapshot grain is flight/service-date and refreshes the current service-date aggregate. A crew bridge is a future requirement only; no crew source or operational bridge is implemented. Airport dimension demonstrates SCD2; aircraft SCD1+3; passenger loyalty is SCD1. Source keys make reloads idempotent. Synthetic identities are non-personal.
